# Privacy Policy for Smart DJ

**Last updated:** September 22, 2026

Smart DJ ("the app") is developed by Matthew Scott ("we," "us," "our"). This policy explains what happens to your data when you use Smart DJ on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, and Apple Watch.

## Summary

Smart DJ does not have a server, does not collect analytics, and does not share your data with us. Everything the app stores either stays on your device, syncs privately through your own iCloud account, or is sent directly to Apple's or Spotify's services as a direct result of an action you take (like playing a song).

## Information We Do Not Collect

We do not operate any backend server for this app. We do not collect, receive, view, or store your playlists, listening history, favorites, schedule, or any other data from Smart DJ. We have no analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or crash-reporting SDKs in the app.

## Apple Music

If you use Smart DJ with Apple Music, the app uses Apple's MusicKit framework to read your Apple Music library and control playback. This requires your permission via Apple's standard system permission prompt. This data exchange happens directly between your device and Apple's services, governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We never see or receive this data.

## Spotify (Optional)

If you choose to connect a Spotify account, Smart DJ authenticates you directly with Spotify using the industry-standard OAuth PKCE flow, entirely within your device's secure browser session. We never see your Spotify username or password.

- Your Spotify access and refresh tokens are stored only in your device's Keychain, protected by iOS/macOS system security, and are never transmitted to us.
- Once connected, the app communicates directly with Spotify's public Web API (`api.spotify.com`, `accounts.spotify.com`) to read your playlists and control playback on your behalf. This exchange is governed by [Spotify's Privacy Policy](https://www.spotify.com/legal/privacy-policy/).
- You can disconnect Spotify at any time from Settings, which deletes the stored tokens from your Keychain immediately.

## iCloud Sync

Your favorites, Christmas playlist selections, playback history, saved playlist links, and weekly schedule are stored using Apple's iCloud Key-Value Storage, tied to your personal iCloud account. This lets your settings sync automatically across your own devices. This data is encrypted and managed entirely by Apple under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) — we have no access to it.

## Backup Export/Import

Smart DJ lets you manually export a backup of your app data (favorites, history, links, schedule) as a JSON file, and import it back later. This file is saved to a location you choose on your device or in your own cloud storage (e.g., Files app, iCloud Drive). We never receive a copy of this file.

## CarPlay

Smart DJ supports CarPlay to let you browse and play your playlists from your vehicle's display. No additional data is collected for this feature beyond what's described above.

## Data Security

Because Smart DJ has no server and no account system of its own, there is no central database of user data for us to secure or that could be breached. Your data's security is governed by the security of your own device, your iCloud account, and (if applicable) your Spotify account.

## Children's Privacy

Smart DJ does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13, because it does not collect information at all. The app requires an existing Apple Music or Spotify account to provide its core functionality.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, note them in the app's release notes.
